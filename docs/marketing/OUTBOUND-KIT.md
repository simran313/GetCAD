# Outbound kit: the first 10 US clients (16 Nov 2026)

**Goal:** 10 paying US jewelry businesses in 30 days. SEO and AI search compound over months; direct outreach is what fills the first orders, and those first clients produce the reviews and proof that make the search work pay off.

**The rules:**
- **Use only facts the website states:** $35, first look in 6 hours, two checks on every file, white-label, NDA by default. No invented numbers, no fake client names, no "trusted by".
- **Personal, not bulk:** 20–30 hand-written messages a day beat 500 templated ones, for replies and for keeping your domain out of spam.

---

## 1. Who to contact (ideal client)

| Priority | Who | Why they buy | Where to find them |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Independent custom and bridal jewelers** (1–3 stores) who advertise "custom design" or "design your own ring" but have no CAD designer on staff | They lose sales waiting days for CAD; a same-day render closes the customer | Google Maps; their website's "Custom" page; Instagram |
| 2 | **Small manufacturers and trade shops** that do CAD/CAM for retailers | Overflow and rush weeks; holidays; trade-show prep | Google Maps ("jewelry manufacturer", "casting"); LinkedIn company search |
| 3 | **Jewelry brands launching collections** (DTC, Etsy-plus, Instagram brands) | They need 10–40 designs at once, rendered | Instagram; Shopify brand sites; LinkedIn |

**Skip:**
- consumers;
- big chains (they have in-house teams and procurement);
- anyone outside the jewelry trade.

**Signals they're a good fit:**
- shows custom work on Instagram;
- has a "custom design" form on the site;
- posts CAD renders (so they already outsource);
- job posts for "CAD designer" (they need capacity now).

## 2. Build the list (45 minutes a day, first week)

**Google Maps:** search each of these in the top 25 US metros (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Dallas, Phoenix, Philadelphia, San Antonio, San Diego, Austin, Miami, Atlanta, Seattle, Denver, Boston and others):

- `custom jeweler [city]`
- `engagement ring designer [city]`
- `bridal jewelry store [city]`
- `jewelry manufacturer [city]`

**Instagram:** search the hashtags `#customjewelry`, `#customengagementring`, `#bridaljeweler`, `#jewelrycad` and `#cadjewelry`. Then open accounts with a US location and a business profile.

**Per business, record:** business name, website, Instagram, city and state, contact name (owner or "custom design" contact), email from their site, the type of business (priority 1–3 above), and one specific thing you noticed (a recent custom piece, their "design your own" page).

**Target: 300 businesses in week one.** That is two weeks of messages at 20–30 a day.

## 3. The offer

> **Send one design. First look in 6 hours, or it's free. $35.**

**Why it works:**
- **Risk is near zero:** $35, refunded as credit if late.
- **It shows your speed straight away**, which is the reason they'd switch.
- **It needs no contract:** after one good file, the conversation is about volume.

**For a manufacturer, use a different angle:**

> Overflow and rush weeks, same specs, your naming, NDA by default.

## 6. Tracking sheet (Google Sheets)

**Columns:** Business · Type (1/2/3) · City, State · Contact · Email · Instagram · Website · Personal detail · Day 1 sent · Day 4 sent · Day 10 sent · Replied (date) · Outcome (Interested / Not now / No / Signed up / First order / Repeat) · Next step · Notes

**Daily cadence (about 1 hour):**
- **30 minutes:** 20–30 new Day 1 messages.
- **10 minutes:** Day 4 and Day 10 follow-ups that are due.
- **15 minutes:** replies, sign-ups, approvals.
- **5 minutes:** update the sheet.

**Weekly numbers to watch:**
- messages sent;
- reply rate (expect low single-digit % to start; improve the first line if it's under 2%);
- sign-ups;
- first orders;
- repeat orders.

Change one thing at a time (subject, first line or offer), never everything.

## 7. Rules that keep you out of trouble

- **US email law (CAN-SPAM).** Every commercial email must:
  - carry your real **physical business address**;
  - have a clear way to opt out ("Reply 'no'…");
  - have an honest subject line.

  Honour opt-outs at once and never write again. Add the address to the signature before the first send.
- **Deliverability:**
  - send manually from Google Workspace, 20–30 a day, growing slowly;
  - no attachments or images in the first email, one link at most;
  - SPF, DKIM and DMARC are already set up for thecadpillar.com.
- **Instagram and LinkedIn limits:**
  - keep to roughly 20–30 DMs and 15–20 connection requests a day;
  - personal messages only, no automation tools (they get accounts restricted).
- **Never claim** clients, numbers or reviews you don't have.
- **Show only designs you own.**
