What we are

  • A managed CAD studio: “your on-demand CAD department”. Never a marketplace.

Prices & plans

  • Public price: from $35 a design. The team quotes every order; you never quote.
  • Internal defaults: Standard A–C $35 (first design $35) · Complex D–F $85 · Sculpted G–L from $120, quoted · renders from $15 · file repair quoted.
  • Credits: 20 for $990 · 50 for $2,250 (6 months; 50-pack priority queue).
  • Partner: $1,500/month (35 standard CADs, $45 overage, named AM, report).
  • Dedicated designer: $2,900–3,500/month (test).
  • Prepaid. No net-30 before 3 months. No rush. No free samples (Fazil only).

Promises (exact wording)

  • 6 hours: A–F first look within 6 hours of order confirmation (paid and confirmed), or the full amount is credited and the work still completed. G–L: firm deadline stated with the price.
  • Revisions: 2 rounds within 14 days of first look; our errors free and first; new scope = new quote.
  • Castable to spec: if the file breaks its spec sheet, we fix it free and refund the CAD fee. Not the casting outcome; not edited files; not waived items; not after 30 days; capped at the CAD fee.

Qualify: 4 questions

  1. How many designs or custom jobs a month?
  2. Who does your CAD today, and what slows it down?
  3. What software and file formats do you need?
  4. Who approves a new supplier?

Brief = size + system · metal + karat · stones · setting · references · cast/print · format.

Daily routine (9-hour shift)

  • 25 verified prospects/day with a real signal (15 in December). A + B signals only in month 1.
  • Reply within 2 business hours; 1 hour for positive replies. Log every reply the same day.
  • Hard bounces < 1%; pause an inbox above 2%; Gmail spam < 0.1%.
  • Sequence: day 1, 4, 10, then stop. ≤120 words, one ask (a first brief), opt-out + postal address.
  • Shifts (IST): USA 14:00–23:00 · UK/EU 12:30–21:30 · AU 07:00–16:00.
  • Block plan: replies → research → sequences → replies → research + calls → CRM.

Red lines

  • No invented claims: no “50+ designers”, “99%”, “AI-native”, client names or stats [TBC].
  • Never “marketplace”. Never “Mumbai”. Never “guaranteed to cast”, “overnight” or rush.
  • No free work without Fazil · no discounts beyond the first step · no unlimited revisions · no net-30 before 3 months · no pilot exclusivity.
  • No cold email to Germany/Austria. UK: Ltd/LLP only. WhatsApp never first. Never guess an email. No bought lists.
  • Never share designer names, client details between clients, or internal economics.
  • Unsure? “Let me confirm and come back today.” Escalate to the Client Acquisition Lead.
