# Sales Training Master — Quiz answer key

INTERNAL — CONFIDENTIAL · For managers. Answers point to the module where the full answer is.

## Module 0
1. Modules 1–7 first (product and market), 8–12 before the first message (daily work), 13–16 before the first client pays (growth and rules). You cannot sell what you cannot explain.
2. "Let me confirm that and come back to you today," then ask the Client Acquisition Lead or Fazil. Never guess.
3. Any three of: every prospect logged with a signal grade; hard bounces under 1%; every reply logged the same day; replies within 2 business hours; first qualified briefs passed on.
4. Any four of the ten lines in 0.3 (pitches, brief reading, prices, spec vs casting, 6-hour clock, 8-minute research, compliant email, top-10 objections, discovery call, red lines).

## Module 1
1. "We're The CAD Pillar, an on-demand CAD department for jewellery businesses. Send one design and you get a first look within 6 hours of order confirmation, or it's free."
2. The client has paid and the team has confirmed the brief is complete. The clock starts only when both have happened, so "payment" alone would over-promise.
3. Guaranteed turnaround with a credit; castable-to-spec guarantee; transparent prices; a portal with history and reorders; published plans.
4. Because we are one studio that owns the result; "marketplace" implies freelancers and no accountability.
5. Dental and watch CAD (possible future lines, separate brand).

## Module 2
1. 3DM is Rhino's editable native file; STL is a triangle mesh the printer reads.
2. "Which country's sizing is that?" — US, UK/AU and EU systems differ; a wrong system means a ring that does not fit.
3. Size with sizing system; metal and karat; stones (shape, size, count; centre vs side); setting style; references; casting or printing method; file format.
4. Gaps in the model's surface; the model is not watertight and can print or cast badly.
5. The pod lead (QC manager); never the designer who built it.

## Module 3
1. About 12,600 (E). Most of the ~100,000 businesses never buy CAD from outside; targeting real buyers saves time.
2. USA and Australia (20/25 each). Australia: highest margin and its business hours are the Indian day.
3. Retail (ICP1) sends pause 11 December – 4 January; January–February is the trough, best for pilots.
4. Revenue up ~27%, units down 12% (V): CAD demand follows units, so sell redesigns and metal-lightening changes.
5. Simple A–C jobs (bands, simple solitaires). We win on manufacturability QC, complex pieces and speed in US hours.

## Module 4
1. Casting houses/trade shops/print bureaus and fine-jewellery manufacturers; CAD capacity is their bottleneck.
2. One casting house that white-labels our CAD brings the volume of 10–20 retailers.
3. Angle: "cover the gap while you hire." Ask: "send me 3 backlog jobs."
4. Any four: consumers; Tier 3 by outbound; retailers under 5 custom pieces a month; Germany/Austria cold email; UAE/Saudi retailers; Italy/Spain/Saudi for now; demands for unlimited revisions, net-30 or exclusivity.
5. Junior designers, marketing coordinators, and info@ inboxes.

## Module 5
1. Client brief → Sales/AM coaching → Admin quote → Client pays → Admin confirms (clock starts) and picks pod → Pod lead assigns → Designer builds + 9-line check → Pod lead 5-line QC → Client first look → Client approves or revises → Team releases files → Sales/AM credits offer and next job.
2. At 11 AM: the clock starts when the order is paid and confirmed, whichever is last.
3. Category C: a credit for the full amount is raised and the work is completed. Category H: complex work has a stated deadline and no automatic credit; it is flagged to admin.
4. 3DM + STL; render image; technical sheet; QC certificate (spec sheet); weight sheet; summary sheet; 3D preview link.
5. Name, business, business type, country, address, website or Instagram, and at least one proof of business registration; an admin approves it.

## Module 6
1. "Prices start at $35 a design. Send me the details and the team will send you the exact quote in the portal before any work starts." (Never quote $85 yourself.)
2. We guarantee the file meets its spec sheet; if it does not, we fix it and refund the CAD fee. We do not control the caster, so a casting failure from a file that meets spec is not covered.
3. Casting failures where the archived file meets spec; edited files (fingerprint mismatch); client waivers on the spec sheet; claims after 30 days.
4. Full refund on request (they message the team; finance refunds).
5. Only Fazil, and only for a top-tier anchor account with a strong trigger.

## Module 7
1. About $33; $35 sits at the median.
2. Castable bench-correct files; brief right first time; speed and predictable timelines; clear price before work; responsive communication.
3. A refund or credit if they miss it (no competitor guarantees turnaround with a refund).
4. "How many custom sales do you lose while a customer waits 10 days?"
5. "Use AI for simple bands if it works for you; send us the pieces that have to be right" — complex work and human manufacturability checks.

## Module 8
1. Find the company; check the CRM; confirm segment and fit; find and grade the signal; find the decision-maker's published email; verify it; log it.
2. A = CAD job ad or backlog post; B = new collection, store or custom page pushed; C = custom page only.
3. Whoever logged the domain first; ownership lapses after 45 days without contact.
4. Drop a personalization level; never fake a line.
5. It is a bought list; DECISIONS 1.34 forbids bought lists until Fazil confirms otherwise.

## Module 9
1. Three messages, on day 1, day 4 and day 10; they stop as soon as the person replies.
2. Any five: ≤120 words; plain text; one CTA (a first brief); no link in email 1 except the signature; real name, title, postal address; opt-out line; brackets replaced with real detail.
3. 30 total sends per inbox per day; about 8 new prospects per inbox per day at steady state.
4. 11 December – 4 January (brands and manufacturers continue to 18 December).
5. Limited companies and LLPs; never sole traders or partnerships.

## Module 10
1. Designs or custom jobs a month? Who does CAD today and what slows it? Software and file formats? Who approves a new supplier?
2. 5 or more custom pieces a month.
3. Open; discover; fit; risk reversal; ask; next step.
4. Treat as priority: involve the Client Acquisition Lead, escalate to Fazil as a 20+/month account, consider L4 treatment.
5. Fazil only.

## Module 11
1. Reply within 1 hour; qualify; get one real job; coach the brief; set expectations; onboard to the portal; quote–pay–confirm; first look within 6 hours; approval and credits offer.
2. Which karat and colour; the centre stone size (and side stones); which country's sizing (also setting style if missing).
3. Price in the portal before work; 6 hours from paid + confirmed (or the stated deadline); 2 rounds within 14 days, our errors free and first; the deliverables list; guarantee covers the file, not the casting.
4. Small prepaid first order; two-person QC; nothing final until approved; late standard first look credited in full; file that breaks spec fixed and CAD fee refunded.
5. Reply within 1 hour; separate our error from new scope; get all changes in one list (then get the time from the team, never guess).

## Module 12
1. Acknowledge; clarify what they mean; answer with a fact; give a real proof point; ask for the next step.
2. No free samples (IP fear, low-value signal); offer the small prepaid first job with approval before release and the guarantees; escalate to Fazil only for a top-tier anchor.
3. We guarantee the file to its spec sheet; if our file broke spec we fix it and refund the CAD fee; if it met spec, the casting process is outside our control.
4. "Senior western-jewellery CAD designers, every file checked by a second person; tell me your volume and I'll confirm capacity." Never "50+ designers with 10+ years each".
5. Do not discount; explain the guarantees and offer to be compared on one job (or the backup for complex work).

## Module 13
1. The same day the first order is approved.
2. Review 5–10 approved designs, write style sheet v1 (master instructions), set a dedicated designer and a backup.
3. The same designer (with a backup) works their jobs; designer identity is not shared [TBC for the dedicated-designer plan].
4. Any four from 13.5 with their responses (late first look, our-error revision, casting complaint, surprise-charge complaint, order gap, slow replies).
5. "Do your retail customers ask you for CAD?"

## Module 14
1. 1,500 prospects → 2% positive (30) → 60% qualified (18) → 35% paying (≈6) (A).
2. About 500 prospects (~25 a working day), ~10 positive, ~6 qualified, ~2 paying a month (A).
3. 6-hour first-look hit rate ≥98%; first-time acceptance ≥90%.
4. 25 verified prospects a day in November; 15 in December.
5. Activity (verified prospects with real signals), reply speed, qualified briefs.

## Module 15
1. Accuracy, Honesty, Compliance.
2. Which order; the spec sheet; whether the file was edited (fingerprint); when it was delivered (30-day limit); what failed.
3. Credits, plus a kickoff and a day-30 review; explain prepaid until 3 months and no discounts beyond the first step.
4. "Send me 3 backlog jobs."

## Module 16
1. Any three of: "50+ designers with 10+ years", "99% right first time", "AI-native", a studio in Mumbai, "guaranteed to cast". Say: "senior western-jewellery CAD designers, every file checked by a second person."
2. No free work without Fazil; no discounts beyond the first step; no unlimited revisions; no net-30 before 3 months; no exclusivity in a pilot.
3. Yes, you may reply: they contacted us, so it is not cold email. You may not add them to a cold sequence.
4. The Client Acquisition Lead, then Fazil, the same day.
5. Identities: clients never learn designer identities; designers never see client identities or their customers.
